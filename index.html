Scene 01The differentiator

Not bolted on. Built in.

Most editors bolt AI on as a feature. WeftCut opens the whole editor to your agent — every track, trim, title and transition — over a standard MCP connection. The intelligence lives in your agent, not in the app. And everything you can do, the agent can do.

  • YOUR AGENT

    Your agent, your choice — Claude, Cursor, or any MCP client. Everything runs on your machine; your footage never leaves it.

  • THE CRAFT

    The whole editing vocabulary is on the table: place, trim and split clips, transitions, keyframes with easing, effect chains, captions, motifs, markers, checkpoints and undo.

  • AGENT MODE

    When a session starts, the UI flips into a focused agent mode. Edits land live on the timeline — keep working alongside, or take back the wheel at any moment.

  • REVIEW

    Every agent action streams into a live change feed and lands under a checkpoint — so the agent’s cut is always reviewable, and always undoable.

  • INSIGHT

    Optional local analysis lets the agent look before it cuts: shot detection, silence detection, transcription and scene description — all on your machine.

EpilogueQuestions

Frequently asked.

Is WeftCut free?

Yes — free and open source under the MIT license. FFmpeg is bundled under its own LGPL/GPL terms.

Which agents can I connect?

Any MCP client — Claude Desktop, Cursor, Codex, or your own. Copy one snippet from Settings → Agent, paste it into your client, and the agent can edit alongside you. WeftCut bundles no AI models — your agent is the intelligence.

Does it work without an agent?

Yes — it’s a complete editor on its own: A/B-roll timeline, keyframes, effects, captions, motifs, audio mixing, and export up to 10-bit AV1 and ProRes.

Where does the AI run?

In your agent, not in the app — WeftCut ships no models. Optional analysis like transcription and scene description runs locally on your machine.

What platforms does it run on?

macOS, Windows and Linux — one desktop app, same feature set everywhere.
